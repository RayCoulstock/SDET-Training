# Codex and AI contributor guidance

## Training content

- Treat `training/slides/sdet-programme.md` as the source of truth for the order and scope of the four taught days.
- Keep the knowledge checks in `training/quiz questions/` aligned whenever slide topics, terminology, examples, phase boundaries, or break positions change.
- A tea-break Menti quiz must contain no more than five short questions and should test only material taught before that break. Include the correct answer and a brief facilitator explanation.
- Each phase assessment must contain exactly ten questions, a correct answer, and a per-question time limit. Keep the format straightforward to transfer into Brillium.
- Use NeuroPulse terminology and repository behaviour rather than inventing unrelated systems. Do not reveal instructor-only deliberate defects unless the related activity is intended to reveal them.
- AI-generated questions and answers require human review for technical accuracy, ambiguity, accessibility, and alignment with the learning outcomes before delivery.
