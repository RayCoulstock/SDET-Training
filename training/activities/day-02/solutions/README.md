## Facilitation guidance

- Ask learners to distinguish observed behaviour from a requirement or inference.
- Results involving created records depend on current shared state; compare shapes and
  rules rather than demanding the same identifiers.
- Do not present permissive participant validation as desirable product behaviour.
- AI output remains subject to human review for accuracy, ambiguity, accessibility,
  and learning-outcome alignment before delivery.
