## Facilitation guidance

- Accept a different seam or review priority when it is tied to a specific risk and
  admits what remains unproven.
- Do not reveal instructor-only defect documentation; let Activity 5 observations
  determine the next experiment.
- Restore Normal after every simulator exercise and coordinate shared-state changes.
- Human review is required before delivering AI-generated questions or answers.
