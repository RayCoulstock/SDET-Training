## Facilitation guidance

- Reveal the worked answer after groups have explained their evidence.
- Ask what each proposed check proves **and does not prove**.
- Prefer a defensible alternative over exact agreement with these notes.
- Run browser examples against the **Normal** scenario and deterministic seed data.
- Do not keep the deliberately failing test from Activity 6.
